# Temple Healthcare Career Hub

Temple Healthcare Career Hub is a Philadelphia-rooted resource hub designed to help students and early-career professionals explore non-clinical careers across healthcare.

## Visit the Live Hub

[Explore Temple Healthcare Career Hub](https://nebulous-change-ebd.notion.site/temple-healthcare-career-hub)

## About the Project

The Hub brings together industry knowledge, career guidance, local resources, events, and professional connections. Its goal is to turn scattered information into practical pathways for learning, career exploration, and professional growth.

## Key Areas

- **Healthcare Intelligence Tracker** — Daily healthcare news tracking and monthly industry reports.
- **Healthcare 101** — A structured knowledge base for understanding the healthcare industry.
- **Philadelphia Resources Map** — Local healthcare-related organizations, programs, and resources.
- **Event Board** — Healthcare events and career opportunities in Philadelphia and beyond.
- **Career Navigation** — Career paths across the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and healthcare product lifecycle.
- **Ask AI** — An internal AI assistant for questions based on the Hub's knowledge base.

## Who It Is For

- Students exploring healthcare careers
- Early-career professionals
- People interested in healthcare analytics, strategy, consulting, finance, supply chain, and related fields

## Platform

The live Hub is built and maintained in Notion.

## Disclaimer

Temple Healthcare Career Hub is an independent student-created project. It is not an official program of, affiliated with, or endorsed by Temple University.

Information in the Hub is collected from publicly available online sources and is provided for general informational purposes only. Although the project aims to keep information accurate and current, it does not guarantee completeness, accuracy, or reliability. Please verify important information with the original source before making academic, career, or professional decisions.
